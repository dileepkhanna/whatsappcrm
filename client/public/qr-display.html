📱 WhatsApp QR Connect

Generate and scan QR code to connect WhatsApp

⚠️ Token Required
You need a valid auth token. Please login first or paste your token below.

📖 How to Use:

  1. Click "Auto-Fill & Generate" (easiest) or enter session ID manually
  2. Wait for QR code to appear
  3. Open WhatsApp on your phone
  4. Go to Settings → Linked Devices
  5. Tap "Link a Device"
  6. Scan the QR code shown above
  7. Wait for connection confirmation!
